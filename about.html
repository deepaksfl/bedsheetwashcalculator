Welcome to bedsheetwashcalculator.com—your quick, free, and reliable guide for taking better care of your bed linens.

Why We Built This Calculator

Washing bedsheets shouldn't be a guessing game. Different fabrics—from breathable bamboo and crisp cotton to delicate linen and cozy flannel—require specific water temperatures, spin speeds, and detergent care to stay soft and hygienic. We created this tool to give you a customized, step-by-step wash prescription in under 45 seconds, helping you prevent shrinkage and extend the lifespan of your favorite bedding.

Our Core Mission

Our mission is simple: to make textile care accessible, straightforward, and stress-free for everyone. By combining standard textile care guidelines with smart, contextual hygiene insights, we help you maintain a clean, allergen-free sleeping environment effortlessly.
