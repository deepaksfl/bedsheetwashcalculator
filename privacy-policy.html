At bedsheetwashcalculator.com, accessible from our main page, your privacy is extremely important to us. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and recorded by our website and how we use it.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Our interactive questionnaire processes your answers directly inside your web browser to generate your custom wash prescription. We do not store, log, or track your personal questionnaire answers on any server database.

2. Log Files & Analytics

Like many standard websites, bedsheetwashcalculator.com makes use of log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamp, referring/exit pages, and potentially the number of clicks. This data is not linked to any information that is personally identifiable.

3. Cookies and Google AdSense

bedsheetwashcalculator.com uses cookies to store information about visitors' preferences and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies (such as the DART cookie) to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

4. Third-Party Privacy Policies

bedsheetwashcalculator.com's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. Thus, we advise you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party ad servers for more detailed information.

5. Contact Us

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, or wish to make a privacy-related inquiry, please reach out to us via email at: evolvewithds@gmail.com.
